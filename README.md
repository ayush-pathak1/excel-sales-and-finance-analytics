## Project Overview

Sales and Finance Analytics are essential for understanding how a business performs, where revenue comes from, and how effectively an organization manages its financial resources.

In this project, I developed five analytical reports using Microsoft Excel, covering two major business functions:

**1. Sales Analytics**

- Customer Performance Analysis
- Market Performance vs Sales Targets

**2. Finance Analytics**

- Profit & Loss Statement by Fiscal Year
- Profit & Loss Statement by Months
- Profit & Loss Statement by Markets

These reports provide different perspectives on business performance and help organize financial and sales information into structured, understandable reports.

## Business Problem

Businesses generate large amounts of sales and financial data. However, raw data alone does not provide enough information for effective decision-making.

Business stakeholders need answers to questions such as:

- Which customers contribute to sales performance?
- How are different markets performing against their sales targets?
- How does financial performance change across fiscal years?
- What are the monthly sales and profitability trends?
- Which markets have stronger or weaker gross margins?

The purpose of this project is to address these analytical requirements by preparing structured Sales and Finance reports using Excel.

## Project Objectives

The main objectives were:

1. Analyze customer-level sales performance.
2. Compare market performance against sales targets.
3. Evaluate financial performance across fiscal years.
4. Analyze monthly Profit & Loss performance.
5. Compare profitability across different markets.
6. Present business metrics in an organized and user-friendly format.
7. Support business understanding through structured reports and financial KPIs.

# 2. Finance Analytics

Finance Analytics helps organizations evaluate financial performance, understand profitability, support financial planning, and communicate financial results to stakeholders.

The Finance Analytics section contains three Profit & Loss reports.

## Financial Metrics Covered

The P&L reports focus on the following important financial metrics:

| Metric         | Description                                         |
| -------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Net Sales      | Revenue after applicable sales deductions           |
| COGS           | Cost of Goods Sold                                  |
| Gross Margin   | Difference between Net Sales and COGS               |
| Gross Margin % | Gross Margin expressed as a percentage of Net Sales |

These metrics help evaluate the financial performance and profitability of a business.

## 2.1 Profit & Loss by Fiscal Year

**Objective:** Analyze the company's financial performance across fiscal years.

This report provides a yearly overview of financial performance and profitability.

**Key analysis areas:**

- Net Sales across fiscal years.
- Cost of Goods Sold (COGS).
- Gross Margin.
- Gross Margin Percentage (GM%).

**Business Applications:**

- Evaluate financial performance across years.
- Understand changes in profitability.
- Support financial performance comparisons.
- Provide a foundation for budgeting and forecasting.
- Communicate financial results to stakeholders.

# Tools and Technologies

The project was developed using the following Excel features and technologies.

| Technology       | Application                                     |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Microsoft Excel  | Business data analysis and report creation      |
| Power Query      | Data extraction, transformation and loading     |
| Power Pivot      | Data modelling and relationship management      |
| Excel Data Model | Connecting and managing multiple related tables |
| DAX              | Calculated columns and analytical calculations  |
| PivotTables      | Summarizing and analyzing business data         |

## Technical Implementation

The project involved the following technical concepts:

**1. ETL Methodology**

Applied the Extract, Transform and Load approach to prepare business data for analysis.

**2. Data Transformation with Power Query**

Used Power Query concepts for transforming data and generating date-related information.

**3. Date Table Creation**

Generated a date table and derived fiscal months and quarters for time-based analysis.

**4. Data Modelling with Power Pivot**

Established relationships between different tables in the Excel Data Model.

**5. DAX Calculations**

Used DAX to create calculated columns and support analytical calculations.

**6. PivotTable Reporting**

Used PivotTables to summarize business data and prepare Sales and Finance reports.

**7. Report Formatting and Optimization**

Fine-tuned report layouts to make business information easier to understand and interpret.
